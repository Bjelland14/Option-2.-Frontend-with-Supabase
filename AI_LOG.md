# AI Usage Log

## Tool Used

ChatGPT

## Usage Period

27.07.26 – 12.08.26

## Assignment

Option 2 – Frontend with Supabase

## Purpose

ChatGPT was used as a support tool during the development of the Frontend with Supabase assignment. It was mainly used to help me understand the assignment structure, Supabase authentication, email confirmation, Row Level Security, and browser error messages.

AI was used for explanations, brainstorming, and debugging assistance. 

## What AI Was Used For

* Brainstorming the structure and user flow of the application.
* Clarifying the requirements in the assignment brief.
* Explaining how Supabase registration and login work.
* Explaining how email confirmation works in Supabase.
* Helping me understand why the email confirmation feedback did not appear as expected.
* Explaining browser console errors and Supabase error messages.
* Explaining the purpose of Row Level Security.
* Helping me understand why database requests could be rejected by RLS policies.
* Explaining how authentication state can be used to show or hide navigation links.
* Describing possible causes of errors related to article creation and retrieval.
* Brainstorming appropriate user feedback for loading, success, empty, and error states.
* Helping structure the written reflection and AI usage log.

## Outcome

The assistance helped me better understand Supabase authentication, email confirmation, Row Level Security, and browser debugging.

One of the main challenges was that the expected email confirmation feedback did not appear after registration. ChatGPT helped me understand possible causes and identify which settings, error messages, and parts of the authentication flow I needed to investigate.

The explanations helped me continue when I was stuck. All final solutions were reviewed, tested, adjusted, and implemented independently.

## Notes

AI was mainly used when I encountered errors or needed clarification about Supabase and JavaScript concepts.

Some issues required several attempts and manual testing before they were solved. ChatGPT was treated as a learning and debugging assistant rather than a tool for producing finished code.
